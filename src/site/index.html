    About Curanet

    Curanet collects headlines from news feeds and videos from YouTube and shows them in one feed you can narrow by category, language, location and type.

    Curanet shows only headlines, short excerpts and preview images, with links to the original publishers. Publishers keep all rights to their work; nothing is copied or rewritten here.

    If you publish one of these sources and want it removed, write to the address below and it will be taken out promptly.

    Removal requests: removal@curanet.io
